05 / Findings & action report

Funding discussion pack

  1. Business registration, ownership records, identity documents and required operating permissions.
  2. Customer problem, offer, pricing, delivery capacity and use-of-funds schedule.
  3. Management accounts, bank statements, forecasts, existing obligations and downside scenarios.
  4. Asset quotations and specifications for sale/lease funding; capital contributions and management plan for partnerships.
  5. Draft term sheet: costs, security, governance, reporting, profit definition, loss treatment, exit and dispute process.
  6. Provider’s Shariah approval and transaction sequence, reviewed alongside local legal and tax advice.
